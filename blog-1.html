Hello from Team 2

Oct 20, 2026 · 2 min read

Welcome to the I2T2 blog! I2T2 stands for task Intermediate 2 of Team 2, and this little website is where we share what we are building and learning along the way.

We are a small team, but we like to keep things organised. That is why I2T2 is split into three departments, each with its own focus.

Our departments

  • Human Resource (HR) — takes care of people: recruiting, onboarding and keeping the team happy.
  • Sales — talks to customers and turns good ideas into real deals.
  • Marketing — tells our story and makes sure people know who we are.

What's next

In the coming weeks we will post updates about our lab work, the tools we use, and lessons we learn as a team. If you have questions or ideas, feel free to send us a message through the contact form.
